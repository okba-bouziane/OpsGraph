# Postmortem: SMS delayed by up to 9 hours
*Sample data, fictional.*

Document ID: PM-2025-06
Type: Postmortem
Date: 2025-06-21
Severity: SEV3
Root cause: SVC-EVENTS
Impacted: SVC-NOTIFY; SVC-DELIVERY
Follow-ups: RB-EVENTS-BACKLOG
Duration: 9 hours

## Summary
A malformed delivery event blocked the notifications queue. The worker retried it forever, 210,000 SMS piled up, and customers received order confirmations up to 9 hours late. Delivery statuses also lagged.

## Root cause
The queue had no dead-letter policy, so one message that always failed blocked everything behind it.

## Action items
- TEAM-PLATFORM will add a dead-letter queue to every queue on the events bus by 2025-07-15.
- TEAM-ORDERS will drop SMS older than 6 hours instead of sending them by 2025-07-31.
