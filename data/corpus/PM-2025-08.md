# Postmortem: Card payments refused for 2 hours
*Sample data, fictional.*

Document ID: PM-2025-08
Type: Postmortem
Date: 2025-08-09
Severity: SEV1
Root cause: SVC-NUMIDIAPAY
Impacted: SVC-PAYMENTS; SVC-CHECKOUT
Follow-ups: ADR-003; RB-PAY-DECLINES
Duration: 2 hours 10 minutes

## Summary
Our client certificate for Numidia Pay expired on a Saturday morning. Every card payment was refused for 2 hours and 10 minutes, until the certificate was renewed. Cash-on-delivery orders kept working.

## Root cause
The certificate had been renewed by hand the year before and nobody owned the renewal date. There was no alert on certificate expiry.

## Action items
- TEAM-PAY will add an alert 30 days before any external certificate expires by 2025-08-31.
- TEAM-PAY will add a feature flag to switch checkout to cash on delivery only by 2025-08-20.
