# Postmortem: Orders failing after a catalog import
*Sample data, fictional.*

Document ID: PM-2025-10
Type: Postmortem
Date: 2025-10-06
Severity: SEV2
Root cause: SVC-MAINDB
Impacted: SVC-ORDERS; SVC-CATALOG
Follow-ups: ADR-004; RB-DB-CONNECTIONS
Duration: 35 minutes

## Summary
A large seller import opened hundreds of database connections. main-db reached its limit of 400 connections, and orders-service could not get a connection for 35 minutes. About 900 orders failed.

## Root cause
Each catalog import worker opened its own connections without a limit, directly to the primary.

## Action items
- TEAM-PLATFORM will put PgBouncer in front of main-db for every service by 2025-10-31.
- TEAM-DISCOVERY will limit seller imports to 4 workers by 2025-10-15.
