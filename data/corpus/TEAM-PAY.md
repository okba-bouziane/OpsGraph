## Mission
Checkout & Payments owns the path from the cart to a paid order: the checkout API, the payments gateway and the relationship with the card processor Numidia Pay. The team's goal is that 99.5% of checkout attempts complete without a server error.

## Working agreements
Changes to payments code need a review from a second team member. Deploys to the payments gateway are not allowed on Thursday evenings, when weekend traffic starts.
