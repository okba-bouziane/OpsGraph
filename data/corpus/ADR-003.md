# Decision: Monitor and automate external certificates
*Sample data, fictional.*

Document ID: ADR-003
Type: Decision
Date: 2025-08-18
Status: Accepted
Applies to: SVC-PAYMENTS; SVC-NUMIDIAPAY
References: PM-2025-08

## Context
An expired client certificate stopped all card payments for over two hours.

## Decision
Every certificate used with an external partner gets an owner team, an expiry alert 30 days ahead, and a renewal ticket created automatically 45 days ahead.
